# A Hundred Verses from Old Japan (1909): poet illustrations

These are all 101 files in Wikimedia Commons, Category:A Hundred Verses from Old Japan (book),
at full original resolution. They were downloaded 2026-10-20 and checked against the Commons SHA-1 checksums.
See `sources.tsv` for each file's Commons page and licence tag.

- `poet_001.png` … `poet_100.png` (Commons: "A Hundred Verses from Old Japan - poem NNN.png"; mapping in `sources.tsv`): one illustration per poem, about 1200×1050 px.
- `book_scan.djvu` (Commons: Hundredversesfro00fujiuoft.djvu): a scan of the whole book (232 pages, 1700×2712).

**Book:** *A Hundred Verses from Old Japan, being a translation of the Hyaku-nin-isshiu*,
translated by William N. Porter. Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1909.
**Scan:** Internet Archive `hundredversesfro00fujiuoft` (University of Toronto), scanned at 400 ppi.
The Commons PNGs are crops of these scans at full size.
**Illustrations:** unknown artist. Commons says they were "taken from an 18th-century Japanese text".
**Licence:** public domain. Commons tags them PD-old-100-expired / PD-scan; the book itself is tagged PD-old-70-1923.
