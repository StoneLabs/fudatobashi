# Edo-period Hyakunin Isshu karuta — card images

`hyakuninisshu_001.jpg` … `hyakuninisshu_100.jpg` are the original files of
Wikimedia Commons, Category:Ogura Hyakunin Isshu (complete set). They were
downloaded 2026-10-20 and checked against the Commons SHA-1 checksums.
See `sources.tsv` for each card's Commons page and original URL.

- Size: about 324×459 px; card 057 is 615×852 px. No larger free copy of this set is online.
- The cards are late-Edo karuta by an unknown artist. They are public domain (Commons tags them PD-Art / PD-old).
- The images first came from 水垣久 (Mizugaki Hisashi)'s website 「やまとうた」, on a page called 百人一首カルタ美術館 that has since been deleted.
  No museum or institution is recorded as holding the set.

The same page forbids commercial publication of the site's *texts*
(transcriptions and translations). That rule covers text only, not these images.
